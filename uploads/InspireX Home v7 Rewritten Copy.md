**Headline alternatives**

1. **Win more customers. Measure every one.** Leads with the outcome and ties into the "Growth you can measure" section below.

**Why these changes:** "Predictive analytics" is a feature; "win customers" is the benefit. The logo strip no longer repeats the 25+ awards figure, which appears again in the stats row two screens down.
